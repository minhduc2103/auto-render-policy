Privacy Policy for Auto Render

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Auto Render ("we", "our", or "the Application") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our software and connect your Google account via Google OAuth 2.0.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize Auto Render to connect with your Google account, the Application may request access to specific Google API scopes solely required to perform the actions you initiate, such as:

  • Basic profile information (email address) to identify your active session.
  • Authorized media/channel permissions (e.g., uploading or managing rendered videos) strictly triggered by your explicit workflow actions.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data and OAuth access tokens are used exclusively to execute the automated rendering and publishing tasks requested by you within the Application. Specifically:

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to unauthorized external servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data or Google user data with any third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or unrelated analytics.

3. Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

Auto Render's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Retention and Revocation

You retain full control over your data. You can revoke Auto Render's access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account Security Permissions page, or by deleting the local configuration files on your computer.
