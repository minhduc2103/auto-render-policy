Auto Render

Auto Render is a desktop automation and media processing application designed to streamline video rendering, content formatting, and authorized media publishing workflows for content creators.

Key Features

  • Automated Video Rendering: Batch-process audio, images, and subtitles into ready-to-publish video formats.
  • Seamless Google Integration: Securely authenticate via Google OAuth 2.0 to manage and upload rendered media directly to your authorized channels.
  • Local-First Processing: Media rendering tasks are executed locally on the user's machine to ensure maximum speed and data privacy.
